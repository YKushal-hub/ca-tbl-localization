    Tier escalation¶

    Two rules that must be kept apart¶

    The design asks whether a more precise radio is worth activating before activating it. From the tier-1 posterior \(P_{868}\) and a predicted geometry \(H\) and noise \(R\) for the candidate radio,

    \[P^+=P_{868}-P_{868}H^{\mathsf T}(HP_{868}H^{\mathsf T}+R)^{-1}HP_{868},\qquad \Delta J=\mathrm{tr}P_{868}-\mathrm{tr}P^+ ,\]

    and the radio is activated when \(\Delta J/C>\lambda\) (the sensor-selection formulation of Joshi and Boyd). This rule has not been evaluated.

    The implementation evaluated here is a threshold rule on the uncertainty score.

    The implemented rule¶

    step behaviour
    escalate one tier when \(\Phi\) falls below a per-tier threshold: 1.642 from tier 1, 1.377 from tier 2
    de-escalate one tier after \(N_d\) consecutive cycles with \(\Phi\) above 1.542 (tier 2) or 1.880 (tier 3)
    not delivered if the active tier returned fewer than three ranges, count the cycle; demote after \(N_d\). Demotion is never deferred

    The gap between the two thresholds and the dwell bias the node toward staying at higher precision, on the argument that in a search a missed escalation costs more than a wasted one. The thresholds come from the calibration campaign; the dwell is the project's choice.

    Suppress-only checks¶

    An escalation the score requests passes checks that can only suppress, never create, an escalation:

    1. Switching-point test. The other nodes ranged this cycle are witnesses. Witness \(j\) agrees if \(|r_j-\lVert\hat x-p_j\rVert|\le k\sqrt{\sigma_{\hat x}^2+\sigma_{p_j}^2+\sigma_\rho^2}\) with \(k=3\), \(\sigma_\rho=30\) m. With at least two witnesses, an escalation is refused if fewer than half agree.
    2. Evidence gate (2.4 GHz to UWB). Suppress unless \(\sqrt{\mathrm{tr}P}\le0.25\times20\) m and at least three UWB-capable nodes (gateways or finders) are predicted within 20 m.

    Two different mechanisms

    The evidence gate (above) and the integrity check (a Mahalanobis test of a fix against the prior) are different mechanisms with different measured behaviour. The repository sometimes calls both "the gate". This site does not. The integrity check is off in the preset.

    The handshake¶

    An escalation is a request and a grant, sent at the highest access category. The request crosses the mesh to an arbiter, and UWB is entered only on a grant. Under the 2.5 % duty cap a tier-1 ranging frame of 56.58 ms arms 2.263 s of off-time against a 2.5 s cycle, so a handshake departing tier 1 competes with ranging for the same airtime (regulatory considerations).

    Tier 3 is scarce, so the arbiter scores competing requests,

    \[U(r)=\lambda_1 r_{\deg}+\lambda_2(1/E_b)+\lambda_3\zeta+\lambda_4\mathbb 1[\mathrm{query}],\]

    over uncertainty-degradation rate, inverse remaining battery energy, staleness and an operator-query indicator, normalised before weighting. The \(\lambda\) encode policy, not measurement. The arbiter keeps a queue and anchors gossip their grants every 5 s.
