## Open items

### A1 — the `--dwell` sweep at b3000
**Never run.** Not blocked by anything: no code change, `--dwell` has existed since
before this session, and the runner pattern is the same as every other sweep here. It
was listed in the plan, displaced by other work, and then — worse — left out of the
first version of this register.

- **Cost:** ~100 cells, roughly 70 min of compute. No engineering.
- **Blocks:** nothing. **Unblocks:** reframing the conjunction claim from *fails at
  scale* into *requires a precision-tier coverage fraction above a threshold*, which is
  a deployment requirement rather than a fatal result.
- **Why it matters:** b1000/gw3 and b3000/gw9 have identical gateway density and differ
  8× in median error, so density is not the explanatory variable. Coverage fraction is.

### A7 — a fidelity check that reads the sensitivity tables
**Never built.** Same story: listed, displaced, then omitted from the register.

- **Cost:** small gate, but the negative fixture is the slow half and is what makes it

- **Why it matters:** the six-decibel SF-index defect sat on the tier carrying ~95% of
  active cycles and **passed every gate, verifier check and validation in the project.**
  Asked directly, no check in the project would catch it today. A methodology
  contribution with a known blind spot in its own fidelity checks is weaker for it.

### C2 — the full per-tag refactor
**Parked at `83bb388` on `wip/c2-full-refactor`, not merged.** The peer form is merged
and gives C3 and C4 their substrate; the full refactor is what makes a second
**measured** subject possible.

- **State:** builds, self-deterministic (6/6 traces identical on a repeat run),
  `arm_summary` byte-identical to `baseline_v7`.
- **Why not merged:** all six traces differ from the pre-refactor binary by **1 ulp** at
  the 15th significant figure, on `estXhp`, `estYhp`, `meanSigmaM`, `meanSigmaRawM` and
  one `mahalanobis` value. Cause is codegen: moving state from stack locals to a heap
  vector changes what the compiler emits under `-march=native`. `alignas(64)` did not
  help.
- **Open decision:** whether result-exact-but-not-bit-exact satisfies the C2 gate. Not
  taken.
- **Remaining if resumed:** give tags 1..N their own energy/policy/EKF stack, and emit
  per-tag rows in `arm_summary` without changing the header at `nTags=1`. ~2 h.

### C4 — multi-target UWB arbitration
Gated on C3 in practice. `arbitration_harness.py` already scores
`U(r) = λ₁T_deg + λ₂T_batt + λ₃T_stale + λ₄T_query` offline; the work is porting it

### A4 — tier-1 measurement calibration
**Blocked by a cost nobody budgeted.** The work package says to compare reported sigma
against realised error *"from the existing traces"* — those traces do not exist. The
repository tracks summaries and sidecars; every `trace_*.csv` regenerates from seed. A4
therefore needs a density-sweep regeneration first, which is not in any plan.

### A5 — complementary siting
Gated on A4 by an explicit ordering rule: A5 must not answer a question a calibration
fix already answered. Also needs a `BuildDeployment()` change — the four sites are
hardcoded at `±0.47 × boxHalf`.

### C5 — hop-provenance
**Gated on writing the relayed-ranging error model and having it reviewed *before any
code*.** That model does not exist. Until it does, `hopWeight` stays at 0 — a non-zero
weight on a hop count that is structurally always zero changes nothing, and applied to
an admitted-but-unreachable peer it degrades the score for an **energy** defect rather
than a provenance one, which is a wrong result that looks like a working mechanism.
