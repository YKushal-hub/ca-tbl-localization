## 1. Where things stand (one paragraph)

The finished preset (97 settings, three gateways) was measured in CAMP-FINAL. It was then
compared with every LoRa localisation method the simulator can run (LORA-CMP, LORA-CMP-2).
**Dual-band LoRa fusion beats CA-TBL on availability in every configuration**, and Poucet's
lead over CA-TBL is that LoRa fusion: its UWB adds nothing measurable. Four causes were
measured (N.26, N.25, N.15). Four default-off flags that address them are **built and
committed** (`185d8d1`) and null-controlled. **Not yet done:** the gate (G89), the integrity
calibration, the registered campaign, and adoption into the preset.

## 5. Traps already hit in this environment

- `cmd & cmd` inside one `&&` chain loses shell variables. Put multi-run jobs in a script
  file.
- `pgrep -f '<script name>'` matches the waiter's own command line. Wait on the runner's PID
  (`kill -0 <pid>`).
- The ns-3 binary uses RUNPATH. A frozen copy needs `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=<copy>/build/lib`.
- Python csv writes CRLF; the repo stores LF. Write with `lineterminator="\n"`.
- The network proxy blocks IEEE Xplore, HAL and academia.edu (the Poucet paper's full text).
