## 1. What this project is, in three sentences

CA-TBL — Confidence-Aware Tier-Based Localization — is an ns-3.47 fork simulating
three-tier radio localisation for GPS-denied search and rescue: 868 MHz LoRa (tier 1),
2.4 GHz (tier 2), UWB (tier 3), with a decision layer that escalates between tiers on a
position-confidence score. Every node carries all three radios; the tier belongs to the
*packet*, not the node. The contribution being claimed is the assembly and its
quantification, plus the measurement apparatus — **not** any individual mechanism, all of
which are cited prior art (`RULES.md` §9).

## 3. What happened this session

The ask was "make the demo work." It did not, and the reasons were three bugs I introduced
plus one genuine finding.

### 3.1 Three bugs, all mine, all instructive

**(a) `sendHandshakeFrames` defaults false.** The finder digest is gated on
`finderDigestPeriodS > 0.0 && sendHandshakeFrames`. `finderDigestPeriodS` defaults to 30,
but the second condition does not, so **no finder ever polled for entries**:
`0/4 finders hold the casualty entry`. With `--finderMode=2` (sweep, then converge once an
entry beats the sweep) they therefore swept outward forever and left the anchor footprint.
That was the visible symptom the owner reported. Not a regression — a config omission.

**(b) Positional argument misalignment, silent.** I added `gwArcDeg` and `trailDeviateSide`
to `RunArm`'s **signature** in one position and to its **call** eleven parameters away.
Every argument after the insertion shifted by two:

| parameter | should receive | actually received |
|---|---|---|
| `gwArcDeg` | 120 | `trailDeviateAtS` = 300 |
| `trailDeviateSide` | ±1 | `trailStopAfterS` = 420 |
| **`nFinders`** | **4** | **`finderStaleSteerBound` = 180** |

The run built **180 searchers instead of 4**, which is why it became unbearably slow, and
energy went 15 J → 91 J. **It compiled without a warning because every parameter in the
shifted region is numeric.** Rule for anyone adding a parameter: put it in the signature
and the call in the *same place*, and diff the two lists.

**(c) A comma expression.** A patch inserted an argument outside the parentheses:

```cpp
walker.SetTrail(trailY, trailDeviateAtS, trailStopAfterS), trailDeviateSide;
```

`SetTrail` took three arguments so the side kept its default, then `trailDeviateSide` was
evaluated and discarded. Compiles clean. Symptom: both deviation directions produced
**byte-identical** output, which is what gave it away.

### 3.2 The genuine finding — A.10, third form

`BEHAVIOUR_ANOMALIES` A.10 established that **contact loss is not an observable event**
here: 868 MHz reaches at least 4.5 km, so silence never comes and a silence-based alarm
never fires. `--searchLaunchSigmaM` was built as the answer — deploy when the network can
still *hear* the subject but no longer *place* them.

**On the arc deployment that observable does not separate either.** Measured, seed 7:

| | p50 | p90 | p99 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sqrt(traceSigma)` on trail | 102.5 | 132.7 | 148.2 |
| `sqrt(traceSigma)` off trail | 6.7 | 128.3 | 149.2 |

Sustained-60 s crossings: **150 m fires at t=720; 175 m and above never fire at all.** A
bound of 100 fires *before* the deviation. A bound of 150 sits **inside the noise band** —
an arbitrary number that lands somewhere convenient, not a threshold with a meaning.

**Why:** what σ measures on the trail is tier-1 range accuracy — RSSI inversion at roughly
50 % of range, so ~100 m on a 260 m link — **not geometry**. The deviation does not move it
because the subject stays inside the arc's angular span: the same anchors at similar
angles either way. And off-trail σ looks *better* only because the searchers had by then
arrived as pseudo-anchors.

**Do not tune this bound until the story lands.** That is precisely the move this
project's rules exist to stop. It is written up for `BEHAVIOUR_ANOMALIES` as a third
instance of A.10 and **still needs filing there** (§8).

## 4. The deployment question, and where it landed

The owner rejected both existing placements, correctly:

| placement | flag | cep50 | why rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3×3 grid | `--gwGrid=3` | **28 m** | anchors in the **interior** of the search area. No SAR team carries a repeater into the middle; it flatters the geometry badly enough to mislead |
| ridge (collinear) | `--gwRidge=true` | **1932 m** | near-singular off-axis. Realistic for a trail repeater chain, geometrically degenerate |

**The answer built this session: an ARC.** `--gwArcDeg=<sweep>` with
`--gwArcCentreDeg=<bearing>`. Gateways follow a curving access route — a road or valley
trail, with units at the points a vehicle can reach. Real 2-D spread so the solve is
conditioned, but **every anchor on one side**, so conditioning degrades as the subject
leaves it in either direction. That degradation is a property of a plausible deployment
rather than an artefact of a pathological one.

`--gwArcCentreDeg` exists because of a measurement: centred **north** (the first attempt)
the subject starts at the box's west edge *outside* the arc's span, giving on-trail error
993 m and off-trail 3.9 m — the geometry got *better* when the subject got lost, and only
because the searchers had arrived. Centred **180° (west)** puts the containment at the
trailhead the subject walks from, so conditioning degrades with distance from access.

`--trailDeviateSide` (±1) exists because the two sides are geometrically different: inside
the arc the anchors subtend a wide angle and the fix holds; outside they collapse toward
one bearing and the along-range direction goes soft first.

**Both default to previous behaviour** — `gwArcDeg=360` is the existing full ring,
`trailDeviateSide=-1` the existing southward deviation — so nothing measured moves.

### The demo configuration that works

```bash
./build/scratch/ns3.47-ca_tbl_causal_sim-optimized \
  --arms=ca_tbl_full --seed=7 --duration=1800 --trace=true \
  --nFinders=4 --finderPseudoAnchor=true --finderMode=2 \
  --finderStaleSteerBound=180 --finderDispersal=1.0 \
  --sendHandshakeFrames=true --finderDigestPeriodS=20 \
  --searchLaunchSigmaM=100 --searchLaunchOnLoss=60 \
  --imuIdeal=true --imuFusion=true \
  --vizEvents=true --vizTablePeriodS=25 \
  --boxHalf=2000 --nGateways=5 --gwArcDeg=140 --gwArcCentreDeg=180 \
  --gwRingFrac=0.75 --gwRidgeY=0.10 \
  --trailDeviateAtS=150 --trailStopAfterS=420 --trailDeviateSide=-1 \
  --outdir=scratchpad/demo
```

Produces: casualty leaves trail 150 s, party dispatched 470 s, **0.0 m searcher drift
before dispatch**, 4/4 finders holding a 1.9 m-accurate entry, **closest approach 1–8 m**,
cep50 18.2 m, cep95 320 m.

`--finderDispersal=1.0` matters — without it all four searchers stack on one point and
move as a single node.

**`--imuIdeal`, never `--imuCrossCheck`, for demos** — anomaly A.3: the IMU witness refuses
440 escalations in the full-stack configuration. And `--imuIdeal` is a **ceiling, not a
system result**; anything measured under it is an upper bound on what a flawless IMU could
contribute, never a claim.

## 6. What is open

### 6.1 Immediate, and the reason the demo uses the σ trigger

**`--searchLaunchAtS` (overdue report) was built and does not converge.** It is the honest
trigger — a party is dispatched because someone did not arrive, not because a telemetry
threshold tripped — and it correctly holds the searchers at camp (0.0 m drift). But under
it `finderMode=2` reaches only **807–1010 m** from the casualty and stops closing, where
the σ trigger reaches 1–8 m. **I did not find out why.**

Ruled out: `--finderStaleSteerBound` is *not* the cause — 180 and 600 gave byte-identical
output. Next thing to check is whether the mode-2 spiral's "last known position" anchor or
its radius cap behaves differently when deployment happens later, and whether
`searchLaunchOnLossS = 0` disables something the movement path reads.

### 6.2 Standing, from `TECH_DEBT` §L

**B1 (engine complete), ~1–2 days, no decisions needed:** routing unwired (0 scenario call
sites); depth-2 unreachable (finders never relay through each other, so `hopDepth` is
always 1); Case B/C unwired; anchor tie-break unwired; `CA_TBL_MOB_RECEDING` never set.

**B2 — the highest-value open item in the project:** four mechanisms are correct,
reachable, and have **nothing to act on** — Covariance Intersection 0 firings, Case A ~8
attempts in 480 cycles, Mahalanobis 0 eligible cycles, routing no route to select. **All
four are one fact: tier 1 blankets the box.** `runFloor` is true on 99.9 % of cycles.
Everything built for scarcity never meets any. The fix is a regime where tier 1 does not
cover, and §4 of handoff v18 lists coordinates where each case appears.

**B3 (claim-supported):** requires regenerating the evidence tree on the corrected
environment (K.2: *"no result in the tree reproduces under it"*) and deciding the unseeded
`LoRaDistributionSampler`. Mostly compute.

## 9. Rules worth repeating because they were each earned

1. **An exact zero is a control-flow claim, not a measurement.** Four instances so far.
   Prove the path is reachable before reporting it produced nothing.
2. **A single-seed cross-arm difference is never a result.** Four attribution passes were
   once spent explaining a 5-seed subsample that vanished at 20 seeds.
3. **Null-control against a HEAD-built binary, never `results/baseline_v8`.** It predates
   the environment-split correction and produces 171 false differences.
4. **Check `git log` before trusting any status document, including this one.**
5. **No assistant attribution on commits, ever.** `RULES.md` §2, gate G12.
6. **Nothing from a demo run is a measurement.** Demo geometry is chosen for legibility.
