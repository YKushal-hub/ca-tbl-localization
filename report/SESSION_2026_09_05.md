## 2. What was measured

Five campaigns, 760 cells, all `dirty 0`.

### CAL-05 exists, and the check is a working detector

The calibration the register defers to Phase 2.1 and the project had never produced.
Recall rises monotonically with bias magnitude while precision holds 0.90–0.98:

| bias | F1 recall | F3 recall |
|---|---|---|
| 10 m | 0.035 | 0.091 |
| 40 m | 0.389 | 0.610 |
| 160 m | 0.831 | 0.910 |

**Reliably detects biases above ≈60 m single-anchor and ≈35 m common-mode, under 4 %
false alarms.** Below 20 m it is blind, and that floor is physical: a 10 m bias is
comparable to the ranging uncertainty it is tested against, so no threshold separates
them. `report/CAL05_DETECTION_CURVE.md`.

### The D-1 repair works — and closes the confidence-score question

On `ca_tbl_no_integrity`, all three forms improve median error on **20 of 20 seeds**
(p < 0.0001). `gated` gets −3.799 m for **28 J**; `weighted` −3.848 m for 142 J.
A veto is five times cheaper than a rescaling.

And every form **loses** to the monotone-of-filter-statistic control, whole CI below
zero. The legacy score is beaten by 0.283 on `no_integrity`; the repairs close that to
−0.0095 and −0.0186 and stop. PCS is a re-encoding of the filter's own covariance trace —
good once unsaturated, never additive. **Three forms have now failed the same test; a
fourth should not be run.** `report/PCS_FORMS_RESULTS.md`.

### C1 makes the handshake contend

`mac_tx` +26 201 frames, **collisions +41 %**. All three static arms byte-identical,
because they never escalate — the control sits inside the result.
`report/C1_CONTENTION_EFFECT.md`.

### A0 returns FAIL 64/64, and the reason is degenerate

Robust across the whole κ band. But `dD = 0` everywhere because `D_T = 0` **and**
`D_B = 0`: across 320 cells neither arm meets `(A ≥ 0.95, R ≤ 25 m)` even once.
Availability binds at 0.78–0.90; accuracy is fine at 3–5 m.

The registered rule maps that onto a FAIL attributed to the tiered arm, contradicting the
project's own line that *a criterion no competitor can meet decides nothing*. A fourth
branch is proposed and **deliberately not applied** — amending after seeing data is what
pre-registration forbids. `report/A0_APPLIED.md`.

## 3. The provenance finding

**§4.1's reliability result has no sidecar anywhere in the tree.** Every `dwell=800`
sidecar in the repository was written by last night's campaign; no pre-existing run uses
the dwell the claim is stated at. Running that configuration at 20 seeds gives 1/20 and
0/20 against a published **19/20**, and the energy ratio is 52×/21× against a published
1.56×.

Independently corroborated: the regenerated 320-cell grid meets the requirement in **0 of
320** cells. Two campaigns agree, and share one explanation — the original flag set is not
on disk. `results/p10_grid` itself was **absent** from the repository while being cited as
the source of two headline results, and no gate checks that a cited directory exists.
