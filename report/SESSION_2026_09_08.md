# 2026-09-08 — the propagation session, and what it means for round 2

**One sentence:** the system does not outperform a single band or a fixed threshold, and
the two reasons it could not have been shown to are physics defects that were found, one
of which is now fixed.

---

## 1. What was asked, and the honest answer

> "ensure that the model outperforms any singular model and fixed threshold"

**It does not.** `ca_tbl_full` is dominated — worse on both accuracy and energy, paired
sign test over 20 seeds — by `static_2p4` at every scale measured, by `static_868` from
`boxHalf=1000` up, and by `fixed_threshold` from `boxHalf=1500` up, including in the
brief's own 3-gateway 5 km deployment.

That answer was registered as a possible outcome before the campaign ran
(`PREREG_K1_DOMINATION.md` F1), which is the only reason it can be reported rather than
argued with.

**The one place the architecture is visibly working:** at `boxHalf=750`, `ca_tbl_full`
buys **45 m of accuracy for 1.04 J** over pure 868 MHz — 18 of 20 seeds, CI [+39.5, +52.1]
m. Escalating when tier 2 is reachable is worth its energy. The trend across the sweep is
that CA-TBL's position improves as the box shrinks, and **the regime where tier 2 is
abundant was never measured** (§4).

## 2. Two physics defects, and they explain the negative result

### Fixed: the topology correction never reached the propagation (`d05e6c2`)

Tiers 2 and 3 were simulated in a different environment and vegetation class from tier 1 —
a relic of the pre-2026-09-07 model, where 2.4 GHz and UWB were separate anchors at
facility sites. Under the tri-band correction they are the same nodes.

| tier r80 | split (old default) | unified (now) |
|---|---|---|
| 868 MHz | 1515.3 m | 1515.3 m |
| 2.4 GHz | **1149.5 m** | **156.0 m** |
| UWB | 12.8 m | 0.0 m (r50 5.3 m) |

**2.4 GHz was getting 7.4× its true range and out-ranged 868 MHz**, against ~27 dB of
link budget in tier 1's favour. A three-tier architecture whose second tier out-ranges its
first has no operating point where the long-range tier is the only one that reaches — so
the domination question was **unanswerable** at the old default, and the answer it would
have produced ("use one radio") was an artefact of a vegetation constant.

### Found, not fixed: foliage depth ignores link length (`FOLIAGE_DEPTH_DEFECT.md`)

Depth is drawn `LogNormal(ln 100, 0.6)` with no reference to the link, so a 10 m UWB hop
is charged 100 m of canopy — 13.77 dB where 0.98 dB is admissible. **Zero error on links
longer than the draw, maximal on the shortest**, so it is invisible on tier 1 and worst on
tier 3. About 12.8 dB, a 3.6× range factor. Every tier-3 number in this session is
understated by it. Not landed because two link-budget changes at once makes neither
attributable.

## 3. Three results worth carrying into round 2

**The evidence gate is worth 324 J** and is now default-on (`7ec0d91`). K3, 20 paired
seeds, **zero noise floor** — the static arms were byte-identical to the control, so this
is a pure treatment effect. `totalJ` 15.94 → 339.11 J with the gate off, 20+/0−,
p < 0.0001, of which `j_uwb` is 322.6 J. Without it the policy escalates to UWB 92 more
times per run, into a tier that obtained **zero** fix cycles in all eleven K1 cells.

**The integrity check is costing, and the cause is the Mahalanobis test itself.**
`ca_tbl_no_integrity` beats `ca_tbl_full` on both axes in every cell — up to 209 m and
5.7 J, 18 and 19 seeds of 20. K2 ablated corroboration and forced escalation separately
and together: **none recovers the gap.** The measurement that names it:

> `ca_tbl_full` records **465 suspects in 1440 cycles — 32.3%**, against the 1% that
> χ²₀.₉₉ = 9.21 produces on a consistent filter.

**This inverts `INTEGRITY_CHECK_RESULTS.md`** (8/8 energy, 7/8 accuracy), and the two are
not in contradiction: that campaign ran on the split environment. The strongest result in
the repository does not survive the correction to the physics under it.

**The threshold must not be tuned.** 9.21 is a distributional constant; the fault is in
the covariance feeding the statistic. One direction is already ruled out by sign — A4's
*over*-stated tier-1 sigma would produce **fewer** suspects, not 32× more. Three testable
alternatives remain: A4 does not survive the correction, `Q` is too small for the longer
coasting the corrected tier-2 range imposes, or the NLOS variance inflation is not
reaching the filter.

## 4. The question round 2 should ask first

**Is CA-TBL non-dominated where tier 2 is abundant?** Every cell in K1 sat at gateway
spacing of 675–2700 m against a 156 m tier-2 range, so tier 2 was marginal everywhere and
absent at the top. The trend is favourable — at `boxHalf=750` CA-TBL is already
non-dominated by `static_868` — and it points into a regime the campaign could not reach.

**Why it could not:** cost per seed rises steeply as the box shrinks, because every
gateway and finder comes into range of everything — 157 s/seed at `boxHalf=2500` against
924 s at 400 and no completed seed in 2040 s at 200. Each cell is single-threaded, so the
makespan is the slowest cell and free cores do not help. **Splitting a cell across
processes by `--seedStart` is the obvious fix and was not tried.**
